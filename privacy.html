Family Giving Tracker: privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Family Giving Tracker is a private tool used by one family to plan and record its charitable giving. It is a web page that runs entirely in your browser. There is no server behind it and it has no database of its own.

What the page accesses

When you sign in with Google, the page asks for permission to open one Google Sheets file that you select. It reads that spreadsheet to show the giving plan and history, and writes to it when you record a decision or a payment. It cannot see any other file in your Google Drive.

The page does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, or any other Google account information.

Where data goes

Spreadsheet contents travel directly between your browser and Google. They are not sent to, stored by, or shared with anyone else, including the author of this page. The page uses no analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking.

The temporary access token that Google issues at sign-in is kept in your browser's session storage for up to an hour so that a page reload does not ask you to sign in again. It is discarded when you close the tab.

Use of Google user data

The page's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from the spreadsheet is used only to display and update that spreadsheet for the signed-in user. It is not transferred to others, not used for advertising, and not read by any person other than the people the spreadsheet's owner has shared it with.

Removing access

You can remove the page's access at any time from your Google Account under Security, then "Your connections to third-party apps and services". Who can open the spreadsheet itself is controlled by its owner through Google Sheets sharing.

Source code and contact

The complete source code is public at github.com/dmaymudes/giving-tracker. Questions can be raised there as an issue.
